Jinming Zhuang joined Prof. Peipei Zhou’s lab in 2021 Fall. Jinming's research interest lies in Heterogeneous Architecture Exploration, HW/SW Co-design, Domain-Specific Accelerator Design and Programming Abstraction. 
At Prof. Zhou's lab, Jinming served as the student leader on the "Deep Learning" research thrust in the Prof. Zhou's lab, under which the open-sourced project "CHARM" is developed and released on GitHub: https://github.com/arc-research-lab/CHARM. CHARM provides both the software compilation and hardware accelerators to compile end-to-end deep learning inference application on AMD Versal ACAP. 

Jinming graduated in August 2026 with his disseration titled "Efficient Design and Compilation of Heterogeneous Accelerators for Deep Learning". Jinming joined the Department of Computer Engineering at Rochester Institute of Technology as a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor in August 2026.
You're welcome to check out Jinming's personal research website at https://www.jinmingzhuang.com/.
